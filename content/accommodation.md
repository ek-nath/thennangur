# Accommodation

Lakshmi Bhavan, Saraswati Bhavan, Sant Nagar and other rooms and cottages have been built for devotees to stay when they visit the Swami Haridhos Giri Ashram in Thennangur.

## Lakshmi Bhavan

Lakshmi Bhavan is a large building complex consisting of several self-contained rooms for the short stay of devotees, during the festivals and functions, like Sathguru Aradhana, Guruji's Mahapooja, Bhaktotsavam and other festivals alike. There are 50 rooms and a dormitory to house as many people as possible. During festival seasons, Lakshmi Bhavan overflows with visitors and devotees.

![Lakshmi Bhavan](/images/ashram/LakshmiBhavan.jpg)

## Saraswati Bhavan

Adjacent to Lakshmi Bhavan there is the Saraswati Bhavan Complex. The complex has well furnished single and double room units, for the visiting overseas devotees.

![Saraswati Bhavan](/images/ashram/SadguruBhavan1.jpg)

## Sant Nagar

A big gate and a large, beautiful statue of Lord Shiva welcomes you into Sant Nagar. Sant Nagar has a dozen well-furnished individual cottages where a whole family could be accommodated. It is for the benefit of the devotees who come from far. Apart from these places of accommodation there are also a few rooms for VIP guests in a building opp Saraswati Bhavan and a dozen deluxe cottages known as “L cottages” adjacent to the Meenakshi Sundareshwar Temple.
