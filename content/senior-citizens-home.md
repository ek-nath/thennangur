# Senior Citizens Home & Veda Patshala

![Senior Citizens Home and Veda Patshala](/images/ashram/SeniorCitizensHomeandVedaPatshala.jpg)

## Veda Pathshala

Guruji Swami Haridhos Giri gave a lot of importance to the study of Vedas and the scriptures and encouraged children to study the Vedas. The Veda Patshala in Arunachala Nilayam houses students and imparts Vedic studies to them.

Study of the Vedas is imparted to children at a very early age under an efficient learned Veda teacher. They are also taught English & Sanskrit and given training in Namasankirthan. This enables the youngsters to grasp the various spiritual aspects of our religion.

## Senior Citizens Home

It was Guruji Swami Haridhos Giri's vision to provide comfort and care for senior citizens. The GA Trust built a senior citizen's home to take care of the daily needs of the residents.

The inmates also take part in the daily prayers and services in the ashram.
