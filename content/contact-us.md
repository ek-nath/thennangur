
# About Us

Rooted in devotion, serving humanity — the eternal legacy of Guruji and the GA Trust since 1984.

## Profile of GA Trust

His Holiness Swami Haridhos Giri — popularly known as Guruji — was the chief disciple of Swami Gnanananda Giri of Thapovanam. He established the GA Trust in the year 1984, laying the foundation for a vibrant spiritual and social welfare complex at Thennangur.

### Objectives of the Trust

To spread the spirit of Namasankirtana worldwide.

Created a full-fledged ashram complex housing Sri Pandurangan Rakhumayi Temple, Shrine for Sathguru Gnanananda, Mahashodasi Temple, and Meenakshi Sundareswarar Temple — fulfilled with Samprokshanam in 1996.

The Namasankirtana Mandapam — a great hall — has been constructed for regular Bhajan sessions.

The GA Trust built "Guruji Medical Centre & Hospital", a lifeline for Thennangur and surrounding villages.

A Library is maintained for the benefit of devotees and the community.

A Gosala for Gosamrakshanam, a Veda Patasala, and a Home for Senior Citizens are maintained.

True to Guruji's teaching — "serve food for all" — the Annadhana Hall was built for daily Annadhanam.

## How to Reach Thennangur

Thennangur is a village in Vandavasi Taluk, Thiruvannamalai district, Tamil Nadu. It is 70 km from Thiruvannamalai, 110 km from Chennai, 6 km from Vandavasi, and 70 km from Villupuram.

### By Road

Thennangur is on State Highway 116 between Kanchipuram and Vandavasi. Frequent buses from Villupuram, Vandavasi, Thiruvannamalai, Kanchipuram, Chengalpet and Chennai. Bus routes 104 & 148 from Chennai connect directly.

### By Train

The nearest railway station is at Villupuram, approximately 70 km from Thennangur. From Villupuram, buses and taxis are available to reach the ashram.

### By Air

The nearest airport is Chennai International Airport, approximately 110 km from Thennangur. Cab and bus services are available from the airport.
