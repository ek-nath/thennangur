
# Refund & Cancellation Policy

Please read our refund and cancellation terms before placing a booking.

## Refund and Cancellation Policy

### Check Out

Before checking out, take a moment to review all of the items you have placed in your Pooja Cart. If you decide that you do not want to purchase a particular item, click the “Remove” link next to the title. When you are ready to place the order, please provide the Shipping and Billing Address to tell us your shipping destination and the payment mode. If you do not give any specific shipping address, then the prasadams will be sent to the billing address.

### Review Your Order and Submit

Do a final review of your order before submitting and check the accuracy of all the information you have provided. You can make any changes if necessary. When you are ready, click the PAY NOW button and wait for a few seconds to be redirected to our payment gateway. Once your order is placed, we will send you an order confirmation e-mail message and an additional prasadam dispatch e-mail message at the time of pooja prasadam dispatch.

### Cancellation of Bookings on Gurujithennangur.com

Pooja booking once done cannot be cancelled.

### Refund Policy

Occasionally, pooja bookings or parts of pooja bookings might be cancelled by our system for various reasons. Some reasons could be:

- Difficulty in processing your payment information.
- Cannot ship to the address provided.
- Duplication of the order.

If your order is cancelled by our system you will receive an alert e-mail explaining the reason for the cancellation and the amount paid by you will be refunded.
