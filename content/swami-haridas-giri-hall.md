## Swami Hardas Giri Hall

Swami Haridas Giri Hall, located in the spiritually vibrant town of Thennagur, is a well-known venue for hosting weddings, religious ceremonies, and cultural events. Named after the revered spiritual master Swami Haridas Giri, the hall holds a special place in the hearts of devotees and residents alike. It has long served as a gathering space for celebrations rooted in tradition and spirituality, often drawing people from nearby villages and cities.

In 2025, the hall underwent a comprehensive renovation, bringing a fresh new look while preserving the hall's traditional essence. The interiors have been completely redesigned to offer more space, better ventilation, and improved lighting, ensuring guests feel comfortable throughout any event. The aesthetic enhancements lend a modern elegance to the venue, making it suitable for both grand and intimate gatherings.

The upgraded facilities now include well-maintained restrooms, a fully equipped kitchen for large-scale catering needs, and dependable power backup to support uninterrupted functions. The attention to detail in these upgrades ensures a smooth and hassle-free experience for both hosts and guests. Convenient parking facilities are also available, addressing a key concern for those traveling in from out of town.

Set against the peaceful backdrop of Thennagur, just a short distance from the renowned Panduranga Temple, Swami Haridas Giri Hall offers a truly auspicious setting. Whether you're planning a traditional wedding, a spiritual satsang, or a cultural performance, this beautifully renovated hall is designed to provide comfort, charm, and convenience.
