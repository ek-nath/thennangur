# Namasankirtan Mandapam

![Namasankirtan Mandapam](/images/ashram/NamasankirthanHall1-2-1.jpg)

On the south of Panduranga Temple, a sprawling mandapam has been built with a stage for conducting Namasankirtan. The colourful statues of saint-poets who propagated Namasankirtan are found in this mandap both inside and outside. The saints include Sri Sridhara Ayyaval, Sri Narayana Theerthar, Sant Tukaram, Sant Meerabai, Sant Thyagaraja, Sri Gopalakrishna Bharathi, Sri Sadasiva Brahmendrar, Sri Purandara Dasa, Sant Kabir Das, Sri Bhadrachala Ramadas. The large painting of Lord Panduranga, Swami Gnanananda Giri and Guruji Swami Haridhos Giri adorns the stage. Sant Samartha Ramadas, Sant Chokha Mela, Sant Namdev, Sant Janabai, Sant Muktabai, Sant Gnaneshwar and Sant Eknath are among the other popular saints whose statues welcome you to the mandap.

Bhajan groups from all over India are invited to perform here during the festivals. The bhajans, discourses and Divyanama Sankirtana sessions conducted in the hall during festivals are a sight to behold.
