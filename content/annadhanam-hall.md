# Annadhanam Hall

![Annadhanam Hall](/images/ashram/Annadhanam1.jpg)

Guruji Swami Haridhos Giri gave utmost importance to Annadhana and would always welcome devotees & guests by asking them to have food first and then meet them for discussions. In the Swami Haridhos Giri Ashram complex, apart from spacious halls for cultural festivities there is also a huge hall for Annadhana where food is served to the devotees during festivals and other important days in the year.
